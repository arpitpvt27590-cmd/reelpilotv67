ReelPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

ReelPilot is a tool intended to help an authorized user prepare and publish Instagram Reels to Instagram professional accounts they control.

Before publishing: Review this draft against the app's actual behavior and correct anything inaccurate. This is a practical draft, not legal advice.

Information we process

Depending on features used, ReelPilot may process login/session information, Instagram account identifiers and usernames, Instagram access tokens needed for actions you authorize, uploaded videos, cover images, captions, publishing preferences, and job/status/error information.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you to ReelPilot, connect an Instagram account with your permission, prepare and publish content you request, show publishing progress, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.

Storage and service providers

The service may use Cloudflare to host the application and store application records, and Cloudinary to receive and host media uploaded for processing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Media and records may remain with a provider until deleted or removed by the service's retention processes.

Access tokens and session information are sensitive and should be protected with appropriate security measures. Never send tokens or secrets by email or chat.

Sharing

ReelPilot does not intend to sell personal information. Information is shared with Instagram/Meta as needed to carry out actions you authorize and with infrastructure providers operating the service. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or to protect the service and its users.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You can request deletion using the instructions on our Data Deletion page. Revoking Instagram permissions may stop future access but may not automatically delete records already stored by ReelPilot or its hosting providers.

Your choices

You can stop using ReelPilot, revoke the app's access through Instagram/Meta settings where available, and request deletion as described above.

Children

ReelPilot is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law or Instagram/Meta's terms.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above indicates the latest revision date.
