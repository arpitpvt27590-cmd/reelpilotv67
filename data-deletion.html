ReelPilot Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 16, 2026

To request deletion of personal information associated with ReelPilot, email arpitkr27590@gmail.com with the subject ReelPilot data deletion request.

What to include

Include the Instagram username connected to ReelPilot and the email address you used with the service, if applicable. Do not email passwords, access tokens, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information. We may ask for reasonable confirmation that you control the account.

What happens next

After verifying a request, the service operator will review and delete or anonymize applicable account records, session records, publishing queues, and associated data under their control, subject to legal, security, backup, and operational retention requirements. Media held by third-party providers may require separate deletion. We will respond and explain any information that cannot be deleted and why.

Revoke Instagram access

You can also revoke ReelPilot's access through Instagram/Meta settings where available. Revoking access can prevent future API access but does not itself guarantee deletion of data already stored by ReelPilot or third-party providers.

Operator action required: This page describes a manual request process. The operator must monitor this email address and actually fulfill deletion requests; publishing this page does not implement automatic deletion.
